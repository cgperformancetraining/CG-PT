Photo Consent

Before & after, and progression images

As part of your coaching, progress photos help us track changes that the scales and tape measure can miss. Some clients are also happy for their results to be shared to show what's possible. This form lets you decide exactly how your images can be used. Every option is your choice, and saying no to sharing will never affect your coaching.

What this form covers

  • Before & after images — photos taken at the start of your programme compared with later photos.
  • Progression images — photos taken at check-ins throughout your programme.
  • Short video clips of training, where you have agreed to them being filmed.

Your images are stored securely and are only seen by Charlie Geddes unless you give permission below. You can change your mind or withdraw consent at any time by emailing cgperformancetraining@outlook.com. Images already published will be removed from our website and social channels within 14 days of your request. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
